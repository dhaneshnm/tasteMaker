## The session (25 min) + same-day delta (10 min)

Same structure every time. Do not improvise the structure; improvise inside it.

1. **First impression — 60 seconds, before anything else.**
   Look. Write 1–3 lines: gut reaction, first place your eye went, one word for the mood. (Source: Secure_Film6786 — the information you lose fastest.)
2. **Timer: 20 minutes. Look and write.**
   - Physical notebook. Phone only displays the painting (full screen, captions hidden).
   - Write timestamps: start, any "feels like a while" moment, end. (Source: Secure_Film6786.)
   - **Phase A (~first 12 min): describe only.** What is actually there — placement, color, edges, light, marks. No story, no judgment words. **Phase B (~last 8 min): interpret.** Implications, mood, guesses at intent. (Order source: nilescranenosebleed; split source: Herman's Visual Intelligence.) Soft boundary — don't clock-watch it; just catch yourself if story arrives in minute 2.
   - Use ONE session prompt from the rotation. One, not several.
3. **Compare — 2 minutes.**
   Reread the first impression. One line: what changed between minute 1 and minute 20?
4. **Reveal.**
   Read the designated reveal source (table below). Max 3 lines of notes: what it confirmed, what it added that you couldn't have seen, what you saw that it doesn't mention.
5. **Trained-eye delta — 10 minutes, same day, separate block.**
   Read ONE close reading of the painting from the resource list (Tier 2). Log up to 3 things the writer noticed that you didn't. For each: the thing + the word for it (e.g., "the diagonal repeats — 'rhyming forms'"). New words go on the notebook's back-page glossary.
   **Guard:** the delta never moves before the sit. If you catch yourself reading about tomorrow's painting tonight, skip tomorrow's delta entirely. The looking is the practice; the reading is the calibration.

**Rules:** a session with three lines of notes still counts. A skipped reveal or delta is fine; a reveal-first session is a failed session — log it as such.

## Weekly review (7 min, Sundays)

- Reread the week's notes. One line per painting: does anything read differently now? (Tests note-revisiting.)
- **Blind-spot tally:** categorize each delta item — color / light / composition / technique / context. Which category keeps appearing? That's your blind spot; consider biasing next week's prompt choice toward it.
- Log: sessions done, skipped, which prompt produced most/least writing.
- Honesty check: sitting because the protocol says so, or would you have quit without it? Write it down — **that IS the solo-uptake question, n=1.**

## Kill/adjust conditions

- Missing 2 consecutive weeks → the protocol failed its most motivated possible user; log as serious evidence against solo uptake and rethink the feature's habit assumptions.
- A prompt producing near-zero notes twice → drop from the app's seed library.
- 20 min consistently too long → test 10-min sessions in week 3; the app default should match what survived, not the literature.
- Delta step consistently skipped or consistently leaking ahead of sits → drop Step 5 from v3 and note that calibration reading didn't survive contact with practice.
